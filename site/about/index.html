About WIZ

WIZ is a space for your own expression of beauty — from the smallest detail to a whole new look.

Our story

We believe beauty is personal.It should be expressed, not imposed.

WIZ began with a simple idea: that beauty should feel personal. A place where you are listened to before a single brush, comb or file is picked up.

Every visit is shaped around you — your hair, your skin, your occasion, your mood. Our team works with care and detail, and leaves you room to say what you want, and what you don't.

What we stand for

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Considered
