Booking information

Before you book, know the terms.

How confirmation works, how late you can change your mind, what happens if you are delayed, and what we do with your details — written plainly, in two languages.

This is the agreement between you and WIZ Beauty Salon. It applies from the moment you request an appointment. The Amharic version says the same things; switch language above the contents.

Both versions are complete. If the two ever differ, the English version and the salon's own records decide.

01About these terms

  1. 1.1These terms cover every appointment booked with WIZ Beauty Salon, whether you book on this website, by phone, on WhatsApp or in person at the salon.
  2. 1.2Booking an appointment means you accept these terms. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you book — we would rather answer a question now than disappoint you later.
  3. 1.3We may update these terms as the salon changes. The version in force is the one published here on the day of your appointment.

02Making a booking

  1. 2.1A booking made on this website is a request. It becomes an appointment only when the salon confirms it — by phone, by message, or by a confirmation email.
  2. 2.2Every booking gets a reference number. It is the fastest way for us to find you, so keep it — it is printed in your confirmation email and on your manage link.
  3. 2.3The manage link we send you is private and expires 24 hours after it is sent. Anyone who has it can move or cancel the appointment, so please do not forward it. You can always ask for a fresh link from the Manage booking page — that link is the only credential, and we will not ask you for a password.

03Changing or cancelling

  1. 3.1You may change or cancel your appointment yourself, without asking us, up to 24 hours before the appointment. The salon's current rule is: “Free to change or cancel up to 24 hours before your appointment.”
  2. 3.2Use the manage link in your email. You can move the appointment as often as you need inside the window; each move releases your old time back to the salon.
  3. 3.3After the window closes, the appointment is the salon's to move. Call us and we will do what we can, but we cannot promise to fill a slot that is already too close.

04Arriving late

  1. 4.1Please arrive five minutes before your appointment. If you are running late, call or message us — we will hold your time for fifteen minutes.
  2. 4.2After fifteen minutes we may need to shorten the service so the customers after you are not kept waiting. We will always tell you what we can still do before we begin.
  3. 4.3If you arrive more than thirty minutes late we may have to rebook you, because the time is no longer there to give.

05If you do not come

  1. 5.1A no-show is an appointment nobody attended and nobody cancelled. It costs the stylist the hour and the salon the customer who could have had it.
  2. 5.2After two no-shows we may ask you to confirm by phone on the day before we hold another long appointment.

06When the salon has to change something

  1. 6.1Sometimes we have to move an appointment: a stylist falls ill, water or power goes off, a closure is declared. We will contact you on the number you gave us as early as we can.
  2. 6.2You may then take a new time, or cancel without any obligation. Neither choice will be held against you.

07Health, allergies and patch tests

  1. 7.1Please tell us before your appointment about allergies, skin conditions, pregnancy, recent surgery, medication or anything that changes how a product might affect you. It is not a formality.
  2. 7.2For colour and chemical services we may ask for a patch test at least 48 hours before your first appointment with a new product. We will not proceed without one if we believe it is needed.
  3. 7.3We follow the manufacturer's instructions for every product. If a service cannot be done safely we will say so and suggest something else — the answer may be no.

08Children and guests

  1. 8.1The salon is a calm place with hot tools, chemicals and sharp implements. Please come with whoever you need, but children must stay beside you and are your responsibility.
  2. 8.2If you are bringing a guest to a long appointment, please tell us when you book so we can seat them properly.

09Photography and social media

  1. 9.1We photograph finished work for our own portfolio and social media. We will always ask first, and we will not photograph your face or publish anything that identifies you without your clear permission.
  2. 9.2Saying no changes nothing about your appointment or how you are treated. If you change your mind later, tell us and we will remove the picture.

10Your information

  1. 10.1We keep your name, phone number, email address (if you gave one) and the notes needed to do your service. We use them to confirm bookings, remind you, and keep a record of anything that affects your hair or skin.
  2. 10.2We do not sell your details and we do not share them, except with the services that send our email — which only carry the message, not a marketing list.
  3. 10.3You may ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Ask at the salon or use the contact details at the foot of this page.

11If something is not right

  1. 11.1Tell us within seven days if you are not happy with a service. Bring your reference if you have it and show us what concerns you — we need to see the work to put it right.
  2. 11.2We will offer a correction, or a redo of the service concerned, where that is the fair answer. What we will not do is argue about it.

12The law that applies

  1. 12.1These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia. If a dispute cannot be settled between us, it belongs to the courts of Addis Ababa.
  2. 12.2If one clause here is found to be unenforceable, the rest of the terms still stand.
